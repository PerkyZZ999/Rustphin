Devices: the three drafts in one

The places panel at its default 240 px, in four states. Each drive's icon sits inside a ring of its used space (draft 2); beside it the name and what is free with the filesystem (drafts 2 and 3); choosing a drive opens its details under it (draft 1); removable drives have their own group with Eject and Mount on the row (draft 3).

What this changes from today's drive cards

  • Smaller: 44 px rows instead of 56 px cards; the bar and the "used / total" figures move into the ring and the details.
  • What is free first: the line under the name says what is free and the filesystem, which is what deciding where to copy needs.
  • Removable apart: USB sticks, cards and phones in their own group under the fixed drives, with Eject on the row; unmounted volumes show Mount on the row instead of only in the menu.
  • Details on demand: choosing a drive shows its details in place, without opening the Properties dialog.
  • Kept: 2 px corners, the progress colour for usage (D-039) and the red from 90 % used, the drive menu, and Units from Settings, Views.
